WHY MINOTAUR?

The body of an LLM. The head of Jev.

Minotaur is a browser agent with two parts working together. A language model plans, reads, and writes. Jev, TypeSafe’s decision model, chooses the next browser action and its target from the elements on the page. Together, they turn your goal into steps through the maze of the web.
